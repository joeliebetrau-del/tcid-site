Submission Template

How the template works.

The structured template is the fastest path to TCID identification. Fill in the fields, submit, get your file back with TCIDs populated.

01

Download

Download the Excel template. It includes dropdown validation for key fields and three example cards to show the format. Submit up to 1,000 cards.

02

Fill In

Enter your card inventory. Required fields are highlighted blue. Optional fields are highlighted gray.

03

Submit by Email

Email your completed template to info@tradingcardid.org. We process submissions and return your results by email.

04

Review Results

Your file comes back with TCID, Match Status, and Confidence for every row. Filter by Match Status, "Exact" and "High Confidence" rows are done. "Review Required" rows need a second look and can be resubmitted.
